Why this exists

I was diagnosed with mantle cell lymphoma in 2019. I'm doing well now, and I haven't forgotten how heavy those days felt, for me and for the people around me.

What Helped Me Through is built on a simple belief: even small things can make a difference in a hard season. Everything here is made for people in treatment and for the people who love them.
